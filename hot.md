# Hot cache

A short, dated snapshot of what is active right now. Regenerated by
`/daily-update`; if it disagrees with `[[PLAN]]` or `[[MEMORY]]`, those two win.

## 2026-10-04

**The vault became a fabric.** One `worker` vault split into `contracts/` +
`shared/` + `peer-node/` + `master-node/`. Most confusion from here comes from
reading a page that belongs to one role as though it described both.

Active:

- `PLAN.md` Step 4 — commit and push the split. Steps 1–3 are done.
- `master-node/plans/MASTER_BUILD_PLAN.md` Step 1 — a real Master keypair and the
  registry it signs. Everything the Master can do is blocked behind it.
- `master-node` Steps 8–9 — cluster KillSwitch and the pooling scheduler.

Just decided:

- A role subvault alone is **not** a deployment. A peer cannot verify a signature
  without `contracts/` and `shared/`, so a bare copy fails validation by design.
- The wiki suite is **vendored** (31 skills, MIT). No global skill farm, so a node
  runs the whole suite from a bare clone.
- `make bundle-verify` is the honesty check: it extracts a bundle somewhere else
  and runs it there. Run it after any structural change.
- An `.env` naming a directory that is not this vault is **stale**, not user
  intent, and `env-init` now regenerates it rather than preserving it.

Two deliberate gaps, both named rather than hidden:

- The Master holds **no usable signing key**, so `guard-apply` cannot be granted to
  anyone and the shipped registry is empty on purpose.
- Live WireGuard, nftables, `chattr +i`, systemd and guard-apply are **unverified**
  on a development host. Designed, not demonstrated.

Still outstanding, not a task anyone has claimed: the exposed GitHub OAuth token
needs rotating and revoking.
