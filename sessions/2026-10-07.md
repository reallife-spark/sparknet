## Gotchas / decisions

- A background job started inside `$(...)` is a child of the *subshell*, not the
  shell — `wait $pid` then says "not a child". Launch background jobs directly
  (env-prefix + `&`), never through command substitution.
- The peer registry client is a two-line stub by design; peer-side *verification*
  is done via `authz.verify_signature` against `root["master_pubkey"]` — the
  rehearsal drives that directly, not through a script.
- The guard is deliberately not part of the peer-only phase: its standalone
  refusal is overlay-reachability-based, not root-based.
- The rehearsal grants `guard-apply` (a.k.a. the sensitive capability), not
  `guard-read` — the vocabulary has no such word.
