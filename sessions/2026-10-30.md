2. **Blocking lint.** `shared/sparknet-registry-install.py` failed `SIM115`
   (`NamedTemporaryFile` not in a context manager); `make format-check` then
   wanted five files reformatted. Atomic write restructured, all formatted.
3. **The re-delivery timer could never be green.** The guard refused every
   sequence not greater than the installed one, so after a successful publish
   the next 60 s tick re-pushed the same publication and got
   `409 stale_publication`, leaving `sparknet-registry-deliver.service` red on
   every tick. New rule in the one shared acceptance rule:
   - lower sequence -> `409 stale_publication` (rollback),
   - equal sequence + equal `registry_sha256` -> `REGISTRY_ALREADY_INSTALLED`,
     nothing written (idempotent re-push),
   - equal sequence + different digest -> `409 stale_publication` (fork).
   The guard logs `registry_unchanged`; the offline client says
   "already installed: sequence N (nothing written)".
4. **Self-delivery to the anchor.** The deliverer targeted every registry
   entry, including one for the Master at `10.100.0.1` (reserved anchor, no
   guard there). It now reads `contracts/overlay.json` and skips that address
   with `skipped: master_anchor`, keeping `ok: true`.
