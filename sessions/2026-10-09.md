### Version-aware compat (precondition for deploy)

The deploy would have **taken the live signer down**: Step 16's schema 1.1 makes
`previous_root_id` a signed root field, but the live root is `"version": "1.0"`
without it, and the signer `verify_root`s at startup → `ConsensusError` → refuse
to start. Defaulting `""` would change the signed bytes/co-signatures. Fix
`49b715a`: `LEGACY_SCHEMA_VERSION="1.0"`, `ROOT_SIGNED_FIELDS_V1`,
`_root_signed_fields(root)`; `canonical_root` projects 1.0 with legacy fields
(id preserved) and 1.1 with the field; `.get` guards in `verify_root` /
`verify_chain` / `check_not_reverted`. Live root: `verify_root → (True,'ok')`,
`root_id` unchanged. Contracts + 2 tests. Gates green.

so Step 20's `except TimeoutError` missed it: the silent client's timeout escaped,

`{"ok":false,"error":"timeout"}`. Lesson: preflight on the **target** interpreter,
