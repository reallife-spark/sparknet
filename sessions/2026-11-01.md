# 2026-11-01 — Qdrant replacement, uplift and distillation scaffold

## Goal

Replace ChromaDB with Qdrant without losing vector functionality, upload the
vector store and KV data to the Master, let the Master build a same-family
student, and keep a Qdrant mirror of every user and model used on a node.

## Decisions

- Qdrant server per node, loopback only (6333/6334); the only fabric-facing
  vector surface remains the Master's signed 4223 listener.
- Full vectors and traces are Master-readable under the new peer-held
  `model-uplift` capability. The Master stores them as training/backup data and
  never serves them on 4223 or to a user AI.
- Raw KV tensors live in a content-addressed artifact store, not in Qdrant
  payloads; capture is sampled/opt-in because a 64-layer/8-KV-head/128-dim
  model is roughly 256 KiB per token.
- Distillation uses traces/logits; raw KV is archived for architecture-
  compatible research. Training is operator-triggered, not automatic.
- Retention: snapshots/traces 30 days, KV 7 days unless pinned, datasets 180
  days, retired Chroma volumes 14 days.
- 8091 is the peer→Master uplift intake; 4223 serves only `sparknet-fabric`
  and `sparknet-shared`.
