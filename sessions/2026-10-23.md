## Decision / finding

The broker receives **one query+chunk per call**, so its `local-rerank` has no
candidate distances and is lexical only. It cannot reproduce 64E's
distance-aware batch rerank (0.786); assist through the broker would regress by
0.014. Keep:

- in-process chroma reranker: **assist**
- broker and local-LLM paths: **shadow**
