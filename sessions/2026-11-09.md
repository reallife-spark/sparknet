# 2026-11-09 — Understand-Anything + Matt Pocock integration (Step 91)

## Objective

Port Understand-Anything to Python/Go, add its skills and console surface, and
integrate the Matt Pocock engineering skills with a local issue tracker whose
work items reach the Master over a signed route.

## Decisions

- Hybrid Python + Go: Python owns parsing/merging; Go owns walk, fingerprints,
  store, query, diff, export and the stdio MCP surface.
- Native-first extractors (`ast`, shell, markdown, JSON/YAML/TOML, config) with
  a generic tree-sitter adapter under the optional `understand` extra.
- HTML/CSS-first console tab; a small vanilla JS module renders the inline SVG.
- Three opencode workspaces: root, `peer-node`, `master-node`; goagx is generated.
- New signed `POST /api/v1/issues` route on 8090 under the peer-held
  `issue-report` capability. Telemetry's closed kind vocabulary is unchanged.
- Matt Pocock family is vendored into `.opencode/skills` and propagated by the
  role catalog; the wiki suite and the new families share `.families.json`.
