## Design corrections from measurement

- The 3B model treated an explicit `none` option as an attractor. `none` was
  removed from the model's option set; abstention is now the policy's decision
  over the distribution plus the deterministic `forbid` list.
- The model copies a template `0.0` confidence. The verifier floors the reported
  confidence at the top probability; the distribution is the signal.
- A sparse distribution is accepted only when it names the chosen plan and sums
  to one; omitted plans become zero. A truncated distribution is refused.
