## Step 40 (D11–D13) — split, in progress

- **D11 dropped by decision.** A `shared/sparknet/` importable package with
  kebab-case shims would touch ~103 call sites and reintroduce a second name per
  module, fighting the by-path / one-copy / relocatable discipline pinned by
  `tests/test_contracts.py::test_only_one_authz_implementation_exists` and
  `test_shared_modules_share_one_loader_bootstrap`. Behavior-preserving splits
  instead.
- **D12a done — YAML-subset parser extracted.** The frontmatter parser + emitter
  (lines 247–604 of `shared/sparknet-provenance.py`) and the
  `ProvenanceError`/`FrontmatterError` pair moved into a new
  `shared/sparknet-yaml.py`; provenance shrank 2952 → 2603 lines. Provenance
  loads it **by path** via `shared/sparknet_loader.py` and re-exports
  `ProvenanceError`, `FrontmatterError`, `parse_yaml_subset`, `emit_yaml`,
  `_split_frontmatter`, so every caller is unchanged (incl.
  `tests/test_contracts.py`'s `provenance.parse_yaml_subset`).
- **Why the errors live in the yaml module.** `FrontmatterError` must stay a
  subclass of `ProvenanceError` (it is raised at `tracking_of` and caught by
  several `except ProvenanceError` handlers), while the parser must load *before*
  provenance exists — a cycle otherwise. Putting the base in the lower module
  resolves both; provenance re-exports keep the public names and identity
  (`provenance.ProvenanceError is sparknet_yaml.ProvenanceError`).
